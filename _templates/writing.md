Open with the problem, tension, or observation that makes the article worth reading.

## The central idea

State the argument clearly. A reader should understand what you believe and why it matters.

## Evidence from practice

Use an example, experiment, or experience. Separate public facts from inference and protect confidential information.

## The trade-offs

Explain when the idea applies, when it does not, and what competing constraints matter.

## A practical framework

Give the reader questions, steps, or a mental model they can use.

## Closing reflection

End with the lesson rather than a generic engagement question.

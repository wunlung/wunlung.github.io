FocusKiwi is a local practice game for children and families. If you need help, email [focuskiwi.support@gmail.com](mailto:focuskiwi.support@gmail.com).

Please do **not** send a child's name, age, exercise history, screenshots containing personal details, or other sensitive information. A general description of the problem, your device model, and iOS version should be enough to get started.

## Troubleshooting

Before contacting support, try these steps:

1. Close FocusKiwi and open it again.
2. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
3. Check that your device is running the latest available iOS update.
4. Check the App Store for a FocusKiwi update.
5. If the issue continues, email support with a brief description of what happened and the steps that led to it.

Deleting the app also deletes FocusKiwi data stored on that device. Only reinstall the app if you are comfortable losing local profiles, settings, exercise history, points, streaks, and badges.

## Compatibility

The current version of FocusKiwi supports **iOS 17.0 or later**.

Pussycat Party

Support

Pussycat Party is an offline collection of five short games for children and families.

Common questions

How do I turn off music, sounds, or haptics?

Open the gear button on the main screen, then use the Music, Sound, and Haptics switches.

Where is progress stored?

Purr Coins, collector badges, selected levels, and gameplay progress are stored locally on the device.

Does the app require internet access or an account?

No. The games work offline and no account is required.

How do I reset progress?

The initial release does not include an in-app reset button. Deleting and reinstalling the app may remove locally stored progress.

Contact

When reporting a problem, include the device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include personal information about a child.

# Pussycat Party Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

Pussycat Party is a children’s game designed to work entirely on the device.

## Information we collect

Pussycat Party does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device information. The app does not require an account and does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, social networking, or tracking technology.

## Information stored on the device

The app stores game settings, earned Purr Coins, unlocked badges, level progress, and gameplay statistics locally on the device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information.

## Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children and families. It does not request a child’s name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or other personal information. It does not provide chat, public profiles, purchases, or links to external services within the child-facing experience.

## Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the [Pussycat Party support page](support/).

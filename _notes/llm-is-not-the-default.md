---
title: "An LLM is not the default answer"
description: "Start with the constraints, then choose the simplest approach that satisfies them."
date: 2026-07-24
---

The question is rarely “Which LLM should we use?” The better question is “What is the simplest system that meets the required quality, cost, latency, and risk?”

Sometimes that is an LLM. Sometimes it is retrieval, a fine-tuned classifier, a deterministic rule, or no model at all. Starting with the constraint keeps the architecture connected to the outcome.

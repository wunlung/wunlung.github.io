# Hi, I’m Wun Lung Wong.

I’m an AI engineering leader based in Sydney, Australia. I build human-centred AI systems and lead technical teams through the uncertainty between an idea and a dependable production outcome.

The thread connecting that work is systems thinking. A useful AI product is never just a model: it is the problem definition, data, evaluation, governance, product experience, operational feedback, and the team responsible for making it dependable.

## How I think about the work

I care less about whether a system uses the newest model and more about whether it solves the right problem reliably. In production, quality is only one part of the design. Cost, latency, safety, feedback, and adoption matter just as much.

As a leader, I try to create clarity around purpose and quality, give engineers meaningful autonomy, and make learning from experiments explicit. I prefer small, testable increments and direct feedback from the people who will use what we build.

## Learn to earn to return

Learning first helped me create opportunities for myself. It keeps me relevant in a field where knowledge has a short half-life, but its highest value is what it lets me return to others. I try to create practical opportunities for interns and graduates, share industry lessons with university students, and support emerging talent through mentoring, hackathons, and datathons.

I have delivered guest lectures on enterprise AI and machine-learning delivery at UNSW and AGSM, and served as a judge for student hackathons and the InterUni Datathon. Teaching is useful in both directions: explaining an idea exposes what I do not yet understand, while students often ask the questions that experienced practitioners have stopped asking.

I write here about production AI, technical leadership, delivery, and the mental models that help me reason about complex systems.

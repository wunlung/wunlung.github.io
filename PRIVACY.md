# FocusKiwi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

FocusKiwi is a local practice game for children and families. This policy describes the current application in this repository.

## Data stored by the app

FocusKiwi can store the following information locally on the device:

- A child-selected display name
- An optional broad age group and avatar choice
- Parent-controlled practice settings
- Exercise results, including dates, timing, accuracy, and game configuration
- Points, streaks, daily goals, sessions, and earned badges

An exact birth date is not requested.

## Data collection and sharing

FocusKiwi does not transmit app data to the developer or a third party. It has no accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs, or in-app purchases. App data remains in the app's local SwiftData store on the device.

Apple may independently process App Store purchase, download, crash, or diagnostic information under Apple's own terms and the device owner's settings. That information is not integrated into the FocusKiwi app.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until a parent deletes a child and their history, erases all app data from Parent Settings, or removes the app from the device. Erasing all data creates a new empty default profile so the app remains usable.

## Children and parental controls

FocusKiwi is designed for use with parent or guardian involvement. The arithmetic adult check separates child-facing activities from profile, privacy, deletion, and configuration controls. It is not secure authentication and the app should not be used to store sensitive information.

## Educational and medical disclaimer

FocusKiwi provides practice games. It does not diagnose, treat, or promise improvement in any medical, reading, learning, or attention condition.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, email [focuskiwi.support@gmail.com](mailto:focuskiwi.support@gmail.com). Please do not include a child's name, age, exercise history, or other sensitive information in your message.
